# Berth: what it does

The canonical feature list for pitches, brand videos and other content. Every
line says whether it is live or coming. Content made from this file must not
present a "coming" feature as available, and must keep the wording under
"Say it this way" (these are product truths, not style choices).

Voice: plain, calm, adult, never clever about money. Names, not tickers.
Positioning line: Wall Street, in your wallet. (Retired: "Stocks for the money that already lives onchain.")

## The value proposition (say this first)

One headline, one paragraph, four points. The welcome page says it in its lede and three short sections (what it is, what you pay with, whose custody); the habit film carries friends and ranks.

> **Buy US stocks with the crypto you hold. Keep them in your own wallet.**
> Berth is software, not a broker. Choose what to pay with, sign once in your
> own wallet, and independent solvers fill the order through SODAX. What you
> buy lands with you.

- **Pay with what you hold.** Stablecoins, ETH, SOL or Bitcoin, on more than
  20 networks. One signature.
- **Your custody, always.** You sign every order. Berth never holds your keys
  or your funds.
- **Start with an email.** No seed phrase. Keys can be exported from
  Settings, Advanced (behind a plain warning, EVM and Solana).
- **Cash that earns.** Park idle money in a Treasury bill fund at the T-bill
  rate. It counts toward what you own.

## Live

1. **Buy stocks and funds with the crypto you already hold.** US stocks and
   ETFs (NVIDIA, Tesla, Apple, SpaceX, the S&P 500 fund, Gold and more) paid
   from stablecoins, ETH, SOL, Bitcoin or other assets on 20+ networks, in
   one signature. Filled by independent solvers through SODAX.
2. **Your custody, always.** Berth is software, not a broker. You sign every
   order in your own wallet. Berth never holds keys or funds.
3. **Start with just an email.** Sign in with email and get a wallet with no
   seed phrase, or bring your own wallet extension. Keys can be exported
   under Settings, Advanced, after a plain warning.
4. **Cash that earns.** Park idle money in a Treasury bill fund, so waiting
   cash earns the T-bill rate. It counts toward what you own.
5. **See what your money would have made elsewhere.** Your portfolio drawn
   beside the same dollars, on the same days, in the S&P 500 fund, the Nasdaq
   100 fund, Gold, Bitcoin or Treasury bills, over a week, a month, three
   months or since your first buy. Each holding shows its own result the same
   way.
6. **The Assistant, inside Berth.** Ask about your account and the market in
   plain words: what you own, how it compares, what moved and why. Ask it to
   draft a buy or a sell and it hands you the order, filled in, to check and
   sign. Facts, not advice. Berth keeps no copy of the chat. Unlocks at
   level 2.
7. **Bring Berth into your AI.** Connect Claude, ChatGPT or Grok to your
   Berth account through Berth's MCP server. Your own AI can read your
   account beside everything else it does (the web, your files, your other
   tools) and draft orders you sign in Berth. Disconnect any time.
8. **News that is about your stocks.** A live column of posts
   on X and headlines, each labelled and kept only when it reports
   something. "What moved" links a large price move
   to the post that reported it. A calm one-line nudge when there is news
   about something you own.
9. **Buy and sell any day, weekends included.** Orders fill outside
   the New York session, through the night and early morning between
   weekdays, usually in under half a minute. Evidence: every stock token fill
   SODAX has recorded (11, 8 of them Berth's, 2026-09-11 to 09-29) landed
   between 01:08 and 07:57 New York on a weekday
   (`reports/weekend-checks.md`). Weekends and US market holidays: David
   confirmed on 2026-09-30 that buying and selling work then too; the prices
   underneath hold flat from Friday 20:00 to Sunday 20:00 New York. Since
   2026-10-01 the app says nothing about US market hours (David: the exchange
   closing is not a thing on Berth); the market calendar stays internal, for
   marks, charts and the X schedule.
10. **Profiles, Salutes and friends.** Pick a username to get a one-screen
    profile (avatar, rank, XP, Salutes). Find a friend by their exact
    username (needs an email on the account), salute people, add friends, and
    see a friends board ranked on XP and Salutes, never returns. Private
    until you pick a name; nobody sees anyone's friends.
11. **One account, many wallets.** Link EVM, Solana and Bitcoin wallets to
    one account; buys land at your home wallet and totals add up every
    wallet. A linked Bitcoin address pays for orders.
12. **Calm email notices.** From mail.berthmarkets.com, when a wallet joins or
    leaves the account, accounts merge, an AI is connected, a friend asks,
    or a limit order fills or expires. Security notices always go out; every
    other kind has its own switch and a one-tap unsubscribe. No marketing.
    Answered at support@berthmarkets.com.
    **Weekly summary, opt in.** Off until you turn it on under Email in
    settings. On Mondays: what the account was worth at the week's last
    close, its change beside the S&P 500 fund over the same days (as the
    chart's one week range tells it), each holding with its price's move,
    and counts of what filled, new friends and Salutes. A part with nothing sure to say is left out; a
    quiet week sends nothing. Always the S&P 500 fund, since the benchmark
    you pick in the app stays in your browser.
13. **Ranks, XP and avatars.** Earn XP for buying, holding, adding an email
    and bringing friends; climb ten harbour ranks from First Line to Harbour
    Master, with avatars and features unlocked on the way.
14. **Invite friends.** Share your link; it carries through any sign up,
    wallet or email. You both earn XP when they join and trade, and you
    become friends at once.

## Tagline

- **Lead line: "Wall Street, in your wallet."** Exact punctuation, comma
  included, one full stop. This is Berth's chosen lead line, used wherever a
  short line represents the whole product: the site title and meta
  description, Open Graph and Twitter cards, an X bio, directory and app
  store listings, and the opener of a pitch.
- **Supporting line (sparingly): "Like Duolingo, but for investing."** Points
  at the habit side only: XP, ranks, friends, learning as you go. Use at most once per surface (one pitch deck slide, one
  social post, one directory long description), never twice on the same
  page. Never in the app UI, never in the site title or meta description,
  never beside a returns figure, a price or any money claim, and never in a
  way that implies streak pressure, a game of chance, or that investing is
  easy or safe. Keep the word "Duolingo" out of any logo or visual use; text
  only.
- **Plain descriptor, pairs with the lead line: "Real US stocks, bought from
  your crypto wallet."** Use it where the lead line needs a second, more
  literal sentence (a meta description, a listing body).
- **Lines rejected, and why:**
  - "Robinhood for your wallet." Implies affiliation or endorsement: the
    tokenised stocks are Robinhood's own product, and Berth is not Robinhood.
  - "Brokerage in your wallet." Berth is not a licensed broker; the word
    invites the wrong regulatory reading.

## Say it this way

- **Don't name Jev to users.** Jev (TypeSafe's model) filters and judges the
  news silently. No "read by Jev", no credit line, no link to TypeSafe, in the
  app, emails, the Assistant, MCP tool text, metadata or marketing. The one
  exception is the privacy policy's processor list, which names TypeSafe as a
  news-relevance provider that receives headlines. Code, env vars, logs, the
  staff area and developer docs may keep the name.
- The Assistant and connected AIs **read and draft; you sign.** Never "trades
  for you", "does it for you" or "autonomous".
- Leaderboards rank **XP and salutes, never returns.** Never "see who made the
  most" or a returns leaderboard.
- The comparison is against **benchmarks** (the same dollars in another
  holding), not against other people's portfolios.
- Cash sits in a **Treasury bill fund**. Say "earns the T-bill rate", not
  "interest account", "savings" or a fixed percentage.
- Tokenized stocks are **tokens that track a share**, issued by third parties.
  Do not call them the shares themselves.
- **Do not talk about market hours.** No "the US market is closed", no
  "opens at", no session times in the app, the Assistant or emails: on Berth
  buying and selling simply work, any day. The only exceptions are honest
  price facts (a chart counts the days the market was open; a weekly email
  says "as of the close on Friday"). Still avoid "24/7" and "around the
  clock" in money claims: prices hold at the last close over the weekend, so
  never imply live weekend prices.
- Keys are **exportable from Settings, Advanced**, not from the account
  drawer.
- Not available in the United States or where local law or sanctions forbid
  it.
- **What we say to excluded visitors** (the soft landing,
  `src/lib/availability.ts`, copy `unavailable` in `src/lib/copy.ts`): "Berth
  isn't available where you are." Then one plain reason: "The stocks on Berth
  are tokens issued for people outside the United States, so Berth can't open
  an account or take an order here." (or, for a sanctioned place, "Berth can't
  be used from a country or region under sanctions"), then "Nothing was taken,
  and nothing about you was stored." It offers the public pages (the welcome
  page, terms, privacy, assistants) and support@berthmarkets.com. Never a
  waitlist, never an email capture, never "yet" or "coming soon" for a
  country. Someone who already has an account and travels is told quietly
  that they can see what they own, sell and withdraw as always; buying waits
  until they are back. That exception is for the United States and its
  territories only. From Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, and the Crimea,
  Donetsk and Luhansk regions of Ukraine, Berth serves nothing beyond its
  public pages, an existing account included: "Berth can't serve this
  place", and "Anything you hold stays in your own wallet" (true: Berth
  never holds funds), with support@berthmarkets.com. The lists are
  `TERMS_AVAILABILITY`, the same ones the terms name, to be confirmed with
  counsel.
- **When availability is judged, for support:** when a buy is reported to
  Berth, not when it was placed. A buy placed at home but first reported
  from abroad (or a limit buy that fills while its owner is away and is
  first seen there) is recorded in full but earns no XP and no hold bonus,
  and a report of the same buy from home later changes nothing. Selling is
  never affected.
- No price predictions, no promises of returns, no "get rich".
